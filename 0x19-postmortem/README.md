Postmortem Report: Server Outage on Tuesday 14th August, 2024
Overview

On Tuesday 13th August, 2024 from 9:00am to 5pm GMT+1, our server experienced an outage that impacted all our systems.

Initial alert received indicating a glitch in the communication between our different servers.
The team got notified and investigation started.
We had to get some engineers on-call to resolve the issue.

The issue was fixed by 4pm GMT+1 and the systems began to recover at 4pm.

[5pm]: Full service restored, and monitoring continued to ensure stability.

Root Cause Analysis

Impact
The outage affected our web pages, payment platforms, registration networks.

About 442 users were affected with so many complaints on our customer pages and social media handles.

Resolution
The steps taken to fix the problem were; rolling back changes, replacing faulty hardware, patching a software bug. Following the resolution, the team conducted monitoring and verifications.

Preventative Measures
To prevent similar outages in the future, we plan to implement the following actions:

[Action 1]: Implement more rigorous testing procedures for deployments.
[Action 2]: Upgrade the monitoring system to provide earlier detection of similar issues.
[Action 3]: Review and update incident response protocols.

Conclusion
We understand the impact that this outage had on our users and apologize for the inconvenience. We are committed to learning from this incident and improving our systems and processes to prevent future occurrences.
